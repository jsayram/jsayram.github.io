Remote Aerial Mapping Spectrometer

R.A.M.S

  • Sternheimer Design Award
  • NASA Langley sponsor

A spectral mapping sensor payload for mounting on unmanned aerial vehicles that adapts to any aircraft able to carry its low weight. R.A.M.S scans its surroundings and computes a three-dimensional map of nearby material (spectral) signatures.

  • Raspberry Pi 3B
  • LIDAR-Lite v3
  • Spectrometer
  • Custom PCB
  • 3D printing

Questions & answers

A little more about how I think.

What are your favorite programming languages, and why?

Right now my favorite language to work with is Java. Although my focus has been Java lately, I find myself looking over to other OOP languages such as C# because of the vast similarities between them. I also enjoy working with scripting languages such as Python and JavaScript.

Why have you chosen this career path?

I have always liked the idea of creating a mobile, web application, or desktop application that people can use to either enhance their life, make something easier, or use for their entertainment. I want to be able to create something that could potentially be useful to thousands of people. Whether it's a simple game to help someone pass the time or a fitness application that's helpful to a community of new gym goers, software has given me the ability to wear those ideas on my fingertips. With the right tools and knowledge the possibilities are virtually endless.
